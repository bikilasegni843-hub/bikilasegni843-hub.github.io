About

Bikila Segni Reta is an Ethiopian legal professional and customs law researcher. He works as an Ethiopian Customs Assessor at the Ethiopian Customs Commission. His professional interests include customs law, legal research, interpretation and analysis of legislation, customs valuation and tax administration.

He is passionate about making legal knowledge accessible and promoting public legal awareness through digital platforms.
